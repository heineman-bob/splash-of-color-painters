Good to know

Questions, answered.

Still wondering about something? Call or text Mark at (630) 779-4045.

What should I do before painters arrive?+

Please remove wall art and fragile items. Move large furniture toward the center of the room or at least 3–4 feet from the walls. We’ll cover furniture, personal items, and floors before work begins.

Do you repair drywall and remove wallpaper?+

Yes. We handle both small and large wall repairs as well as wallpaper removal so the finished paint has a smooth, sound surface.

What forms of payment do you accept?+

Cash, check, Visa, Mastercard, Discover, and American Express are accepted. Card payments include a 4% transaction fee.

Are you really pet friendly?+

Absolutely. We enjoy meeting the four-legged members of the family and work with you to keep everyone comfortable and safe.

Which paint brand do you use?+

We primarily use Sherwin-Williams paint. If you prefer another brand or would like to supply the paint, we’re happy to accommodate the request.
