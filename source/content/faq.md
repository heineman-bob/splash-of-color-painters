##### How to choose a paint sheen?

- Flat – A non-reflective appearance that helps hide surface blemishes, making walls look smooth. This finish, which cleans easily with a damp sponge, is ideal for:
Bedrooms, Dining rooms, Formal living rooms, Ceilings

- Eggshell – A low sheen that resembles the shell of an egg. It absorbs light like the flat, but easier to clean. Eggshell is ideal for:
Hallways, Bedrooms

- Satin – A slight gloss, this finish offers the benefits of richer look. Ideal for high-traffic areas such as:
Hallways, Woodwork, Family rooms

- Semi-Gloss – Durable finish is perfect for areas that get cleaned frequently, such as:
Kitchens, Bathrooms, Utility area, Closet doors, Trim

- Gloss – Very durable finish. Great for windows, doors and trim. Ideal for:
Wood trim, Cabinets, Doors

##### What do I need to do before the painters come?

- Please remove all pictures and art work/decorations from the walls. Remove all fragile items from the work area. Any curtain rod hardware or picture hangers that you wish to remain and not be removed and patched, please clarify prior to the beginning of work. Have any large furniture items moved to the center of the room, or at least 3-4 feet from the wall. We thank you for your cooperation in advance.
